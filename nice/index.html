Calibration Process

For optimal gesture recognition, please follow these steps:

  1. Position yourself so your hand is clearly visible in the camera preview.
  2. Make sure you have good lighting - avoid strong backlight.
  3. Hold your hand about 30-50 cm from the camera.
  4. Perform each gesture as shown in the guide when prompted.
  5. The system will adapt to your hand size and lighting conditions.
Step 1/6: Show your OPEN PALM to the camera
